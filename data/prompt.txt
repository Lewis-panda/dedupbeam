The lighthouse keeper had lived on the island for thirty years, and in all that time he had never seen a storm like the one that was gathering on the horizon that evening. He climbed the spiral staircase slowly, counting each of the two hundred and seven steps as he always did, and when he reached the lamp room he stood for a long while watching the clouds pile up over the grey water. Far below, the waves were already breaking over the rocks at the foot of the tower, and the wind carried the smell of rain and salt through the open window. He lit the lamp, checked the oil, and sat down at the small wooden table to write in his logbook, as he had done every night since he first arrived. Tonight, however, he wrote something different. He began to describe, in careful detail, everything he remembered about the day the supply boat failed to arrive, and the strange sequence of events that followed during the long winter afterwards.
